# Thermal dynamics: rejected minute model and paired visibility audit

## Hypothesis and protocol

Fit small normal-only Ridge models for endpoint temperature change, with previous temperature and current nonthermal operating means as predictors. Temperature inertia and changing load motivate the representation. No current target enters predictors; no future values, cross-run history or bad-quality history. Per-target normal empirical CDFs calibrate absolute residuals; the maximum available thermal score is combined with baseline in three predeclared ways. Quantiles .99, .995 and .9975, Ridge alpha=1, whole-run fit/calibration/development separation. No new test access.

Development: the same 1,965 complete minute windows and 124 original events, including 10 cooling events. Baseline detects 53 events, 2 cooling, with 4 false windows.

| Decision | Quantile | Events | Cooling | False windows | Baseline events lost |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Thermal alone | .99 | 9 | 0 | 7 | 44 |
| Thermal alone | .995 | 7 | 0 | 1 | 46 |
| Maximum with baseline | .99 | 52 | 2 | 9 | 1 |
| Maximum with baseline | .995 | 34 | 1 | 1 | 19 |
| Independent union | .99 | 53 | 2 | 11 | 0 |
| Independent union | .995 | 53 | 2 | 5 | 0 |

.9975 is unresolvable in all three cases. **All candidates rejected.** No new test generated, baseline/bundle unchanged. The calibration and per-event residual details are in [numeric evidence](../../../results/thermal-20261005.json). Large normal prediction errors remain; this is evidence against this minute representation, not against thermal monitoring generally.

## Paired simulator intervention

To distinguish absent signal from an inadequate representation, replayed all ten development cooling cases and then set only the cooling event's severity to zero, preserving seed, scenario, other events and sampling. Every original replay matched timestamps, asset/tag identifiers, values and quality exactly. Nonthermal signals and quality were identical after intervention.

### Provenance discrepancy

Raw metadata declares simulator 0.3.1, but tag `v0.3.1` (`5bf26b5`) cannot parse later explicit process-profile fields. Removing only neutral generic defaults still failed exact telemetry replay; that attempt was discarded. Clean Lab 0.6.0 commit `718babb7772c3a21f0b87c403f628540cbce58db` reproduces all ten cases exactly. The audit requires explicit `--allow-version-mismatch` and still aborts unless exact original replay succeeds. Both declared version and actual source hashes are retained. This does not identify the original generating commit or certify replay of other historical runs; those remain unresolved provenance limits.
